Popposite 1.0.0. This policy describes the Android release and the studio website.

1. About this policy

Poke Stick Studio develops Popposite, an Android number puzzle game. This policy describes how the game handles information and how its integrated advertising services process information. It also covers this studio website. Popposite does not require a player account.

Questions about this policy can be sent to pokestickstudio@gmail.com.

2. Local game data

Popposite saves campaign progress, completed levels, best move counts, stars, game statistics, tutorial status, and settings locally on your device. Local recovery copies of progress may also be retained to help restore a damaged save. These records support progression, statistics, and your chosen game experience.

The game does not upload these progress records to a studio-operated server or provide a studio cloud-save account. Device or operating-system backup may separately retain app data according to your Android and backup settings.

3. Advertising and advertising identifiers

Popposite uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show optional rewarded ads and interstitial ads. Players can choose to watch a rewarded ad for an in-game benefit. Interstitial ads may appear at eligible level transitions. Ads may be unavailable depending on your privacy choices, region, network connection, or ad availability.

Google’s SDK may process IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location, and device or app identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. It may also process app and ad interactions, such as launches, taps and video views, and technical diagnostics, such as app startup and performance information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads and help prevent fraud. Processing depends on the device, SDK configuration and applicable privacy choices. See Google’s SDK data disclosure guidance.

The Android release declares the advertising ID permission. You can manage your advertising ID through Android system settings. The advertising information described here is processed by Google’s SDK. Poke Stick Studio receives the information you choose to send directly when you contact us, as described below.

4. Consent and privacy choices

Popposite uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update consent information and display a consent message when required. The game requests ads only when UMP reports that ad requests are allowed. Available messages and choices may vary by region and AdMob configuration.

When UMP requires a privacy options entry point, Privacy choices appears in Settings so you can revisit your choices. Your choices may affect the types of ads available.

5. Analytics

Firebase Analytics collection is disabled in Popposite 1.0.0. Google Mobile Ads may still process advertising measurement and diagnostic information as described above.

6. Third-party services and security

The integrated Google services are AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and UMP for advertising and consent management. Google processes information under its own policies. Read the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services for details, including processing locations and privacy controls.

Google documents encrypted transport for the Mobile Ads SDK. Local game records are stored through Unity’s local preferences system; they are not a secure vault. No data storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Retention and deletion

Local progress, statistics, settings, and recovery copies remain in app storage until removed or replaced. To remove the app’s local records, use Android Settings → Apps → Popposite → Storage → Clear storage (wording may vary). This removes your saved progress and settings. Uninstalling normally removes local app storage, but device backups can persist or restore data; manage those separately in your device or backup account settings.

Information processed by Google is retained according to Google’s policies and the service and purpose involved. Poke Stick Studio cannot directly erase Google-held information through the game’s local storage controls. Google’s policy and privacy tools explain available deletion options.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use it to respond. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

8. Children’s privacy

If you believe a child’s personal information has been provided to Poke Stick Studio, contact us using the address below.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal information, object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a relevant data protection authority. Contact us about information you have provided to the studio. Use Google’s privacy controls or contact Google about information processed by its services. Clearing local game data and changing advertising choices are described above.

10. This website

This is a static informational website hosted on GitHub Pages. The site source includes no advertising, analytics, tracking scripts, forms, external fonts, or embedded third-party media, and sets no cookies or browser storage. Following a link to another service is subject to that service’s practices.

GitHub processes visitor IP addresses for security when serving Pages websites. See the GitHub Pages data collection notice and GitHub Privacy Statement.
